### 👋 About Me

I'm Anmol Kumar, an **AI Engineer & Data Scientist** focused on designing and deploying machine learning systems that turn raw data into real, measurable impact. As a fresher, I bring rigorous fundamentals, a fast learning curve, and a genuine obsession with the craft — not just the theory.

Beyond building, I create content that breaks down complex AI and Data Science concepts for a wider audience across YouTube, Medium, and Instagram.

*   🔭 **Building**: ML, Deep Learning & NLP systems.
*   🌱 **Deepening**: Model deployment pipelines, MLOps frameworks, and system optimization.
*   📡 **Simplifying**: AI concepts through content, one post at a time.
*   🤝 **Collaboration**: Open to AI/ML roles, internships, and open-source contributions.
*   ⚡ **Motto**: I turn *"it doesn't work"* into *"it works"* — usually at 2 AM.
